Privacy

What we collect, how Dodo Payments handles checkout as MoR, and your privacy choices.

This Privacy Policy describes how Freezestack (freezestack.com) collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services. It works together with our Terms of Service and Refund Policy. Last updated August 2026.

Payments note: Checkout and subscriptions are processed by Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record. Dodo processes payment and tax-related personal data under Dodo’s Privacy Policy. Freezestack does not store full card numbers.

1. Who we are

Freezestack operates freezestack.com—a digital library of templates, motion prompts, and AI skills. For privacy requests about Freezestack accounts and product data, contact contact@freezestack.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Account and sign-in: email address and profile fields you provide or that identity providers (for example Google or X) share when you sign in. Authentication is powered by Supabase.
  • Usage and product data: which pages or items you open, favorites, plan entitlement status, and daily usage counts (for example templates, skills, prompts) needed to enforce plan limits.
  • Purchase and billing signals: after checkout, we receive order or payment references, plan type, status, and amounts needed to unlock access and show billing status. Card numbers and sensitive payment credentials are collected by Dodo Payments, not stored by Freezestack.
  • Communications: name, email, and message content when you contact support.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type, device type, approximate location derived from IP, pages viewed, and similar diagnostics. We may use cookies or local storage for session, theme, and preference.

3. How we use information

  • Provide and secure accounts, sign-in, and the library
  • Process entitlements after payment and show plan status
  • Enforce free and paid usage limits
  • Respond to support requests and prevent abuse or fraud
  • Improve the product (for example which packs are popular)
  • Comply with law and protect our rights and users

We do not sell your personal information.

4. How we share information

We share data only with providers that help run Freezestack, under contracts or terms that limit use to providing their service:

  • Supabase — authentication and database for accounts and entitlements
  • Dodo Payments — Merchant of Record for checkout, subscriptions, invoices, tax on sales, refunds, and payment disputes
  • Hosting / CDN (for example Vercel) — serving the website and APIs
  • Analytics or performance tools we enable (for example Vercel Speed Insights), if configured, for site performance only

We may disclose information if required by law, legal process, or to protect Freezestack, users, or the public. Business transfers (merger or acquisition) may include transfer of relevant data with notice where required.

5. Dodo Payments and Merchant of Record

When you buy Premium or Premium Plus (or other paid products), Dodo Payments is the legal seller of that digital purchase for payment and tax purposes. Dodo may collect and process:

  • Payment method details (handled on Dodo’s systems / PCI scope)
  • Billing name, email, address or country (for tax and receipts)
  • Transaction history, refunds, and dispute data

Freezestack receives enough information to confirm payment and activate your plan. For privacy rights about pure payment processing data, you may also need to contact Dodo under its policy. Freezestack will help where we can.

6. Cookies and local storage

We use essential cookies or local storage for login sessions, theme preference, and core site function. We do not use third-party advertising trackers as a core product feature. You can clear site data in your browser; some features may stop working until you sign in again.

7. Retention

We keep account and entitlement records while your account is active and as needed for support, fraud prevention, and legal obligations. Usage counters may reset daily but summary records can be kept longer. Payment records may be retained by Dodo as required for tax and financial compliance.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live (for example GDPR, UK GDPR, or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export personal data, or to object to certain processing. To exercise rights for Freezestack account data, email contact@freezestack.com. We may need to verify your identity. You can also close your account by contacting us.

You may opt out of non-essential marketing emails (if we send any) using the unsubscribe link or by emailing us. Transactional messages (receipts, security, plan notices) may still be sent.

9. International transfers

We and our providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards (for example standard contractual clauses used by our providers) for cross-border transfers.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures (HTTPS, access controls, provider security). No online service is perfectly secure. Card data is handled by Dodo under its PCI obligations, not on Freezestack servers.

11. Children

Freezestack is not directed to children under 16 (or the higher age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child provided data, contact us and we will delete it where required.

12. Third-party sites

When you leave Freezestack for Grok or another site, that site’s privacy policy applies. We are not responsible for their practices.

13. Changes

We may update this Policy and will change the “Last updated” date. Material changes may be highlighted on the site or by email when appropriate. Continued use after an update means you acknowledge the revised Policy.
