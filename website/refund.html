Refund Policy

Cancellations, refunds, and how charges work with Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record.

This Refund Policy explains cancellations and refunds for Freezestack (freezestack.com) purchases. It applies with our Terms of Service. Last updated August 2026.

Who processes payments: Paid checkouts are handled by Dodo Payments as Merchant of Record (MoR)—the legal seller for the charge, taxes where applicable, and card processing. Approved refunds are returned through Dodo to your original payment method. Freezestack provides the library and decides product access; Dodo executes the money movement.

1. Digital products, delivered immediately

Freezestack sells digital access: template links, motion prompts, and AI skills. After a successful payment, access is unlocked on your account right away. Once content is delivered or used (opened, copied, or unlocked), it is generally considered fully delivered. For that reason, sales are final except in the cases below. We do not refund for change of mind, unused days in a period you already started, or because Grok, SuperGrok, or another third-party AI tool changed its own pricing or features.

2. When we refund

Contact us within 7 days of the charge (sooner if possible). We will review and, where fair, refund when:

  • You paid but never received access and we cannot restore it
  • Duplicate or clear billing error (charged twice for the same plan)
  • Unauthorized charge on your payment method (we will investigate with Dodo)
  • Material defect on our side (core product broken) that we cannot fix in a reasonable time after you report it

Include: account email, approximate time of purchase, plan name if known, and payment or receipt reference from Dodo if you have it.

3. Subscriptions (Premium / Premium Plus)

  • Cancel anytime from Account or by emailing us. Cancellation stops the next renewal.
  • Access continues until the end of the period you already paid for.
  • No pro-rata refunds for unused days in an active paid period, except where we agree in the cases in section 2 or where mandatory consumer law requires otherwise.
  • Renewals: forgetting to cancel is not automatically refundable. If a renewal charged within the last few days and you did not use paid access in the new period, contact us; we may refund in good faith at our discretion.

4. Taxes and MoR refunds

Because Dodo is Merchant of Record, refunds and tax adjustments are processed through Dodo’s systems. Tax lines on your receipt follow Dodo’s tax handling for your location. Freezestack does not issue separate tax remittances as the seller of record for MoR checkouts.

5. How to request a refund

Email contact@freezestack.com or use the contact form. We aim to reply within 2–3 business days. Approved refunds typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days after processing, depending on your bank.

6. Chargebacks

Please contact us before filing a bank dispute. We can usually resolve access or billing issues faster. Filing a chargeback without contacting us may lead to temporary suspension of library access while the dispute is reviewed with Dodo and the card network.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The version on this page applies to new purchases. We will update the date above when we change it.
