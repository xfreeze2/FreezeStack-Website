Questions

What’s the difference between Premium and Premium Plus?

Both unlock the full premium library. Premium is for most creators: 20 templates, 30 skills, and 30 prompts per day. Premium Plus raises templates to 50 per day and removes skill and prompt caps (unlimited).

What’s the difference between monthly and yearly?

Same access and limits for the tier you pick. Yearly Premium is $499 (15% off vs $49×12) and equals about $41.58/mo. Yearly Premium Plus is $999 (24% off vs $109×12) and equals about $83.25/mo.

What are the daily usage limits?

Free: 2 templates, 3 skills, 3 prompts. Premium: 20 templates, 30 skills, 30 prompts. Premium Plus: 50 templates, unlimited skills, unlimited prompts. Limits reset every 24 hours (UTC). Reopening the same item the same day does not count twice.

How do I pay?

Sign in, choose monthly or yearly, then complete checkout with Dodo Payments.
